# M063: A book puts an index no marker names in the same chapter on every render

**Status:** done (2026-08-30, PR #63 https://github.com/jmgirard/quarto-index/pull/63)

**Goal:** An HTML book builds the section for an index no placement marker names in the book's last chapter, so no render can print that section in two chapters or in none.

**Outcome:** `html_book` gates the fallback adoption on `first ~= nil and ctx.position == #ctx.chapters` — the end of the render list Quarto hands every chapter's process, which no two chapters of one render can disagree about — replacing the `last` position derived from `marker_chapter`'s view of the sidecar store, KI199's mechanism. The proviso `first ~= nil` keeps the single document's rule: a book with no marker anywhere grows no section. `place_index` appends the section at the end of a marker-less last chapter's body. The unplaced-section and doubled-section reports go with the state that fed them: `record.unseen`, `record.adopted`, the `later` gate, `store_read`'s unseen pass and second walk, `valid_record`'s two-field loop, and `marks_in`, their only remaining caller. `STORE_VERSION` stays at 4 and the three retired fields are ignored rather than refused, so a record written by the predecessor keeps its chapter's terms (the M14 lesson). `warn-distinct.py`'s `EXPECTED` count moves 79 → 77 with the arithmetic shown. `examples/book-placement/`'s manifests collapse to one — `PLACE_SECTIONS_FIRST` and `PLACE_SECTIONS_SECOND` become the same manifest, `PLACE_SECTIONS_DOUBLED` goes — since the two renders no longer differ.

**Decisions:** D-040 supersedes M55's placement choice, with its falsification condition (an author reporting their book's last chapter is a place an index must not appear). Milestone-local: the fallback section is appended at the end of a marker-less last chapter, the three check blocks whose subject was the retired reports were re-aimed at the section and term manifests rather than deleted, and `valid_record`'s `data.later` type refusal was dropped since a field nothing reads may not cost a chapter its terms. One substantive AC3 amendment corrected the unreadable-record report's cadence — drawn from inside `store_read` once per rendering chapter meeting the held path, 4, not once per index section the record costs.
