# M067: The editor-metadata checks read what they claim to read

**Status:** done (2026-09-01, PR #67 https://github.com/jmgirard/quarto-index/pull/67)

**Goal:** The four M50 editor-metadata readers that misread their input — a quoted value, a construct's term, an unreadable page, a page's own stated count — read what their messages already claim they read.

**Outcome:** Four read-repairs in `tests/editormeta.py` and `tests/editorfixture.py`, each leaving what its check promises unchanged (D-011). `parse_attrs` treats a backslash before the closing quote or another backslash as an escape, the character after it joining the value (`ESCAPE`, a module constant a self-test splices out). `constructs` records each block's offset; `load` reads each `.index` construct's own term off the span it closes (`marked_term`, None for a div) and `attribute_sites` pairs by that, never by ordinal. `check_docs` splits its arguments at `--`, refuses a call with none or with two, and fails a page it cannot read through `read`'s own message naming the path; the four suite call sites carry `--`. `stated_forms` reads "There are exactly <count> supported forms" off `site/syntax.qmd` (digits or a word one to twenty, exactly one sentence, a count under one refused) and `form_table` holds the row count to it; `SYNTAX_FORMS` is gone, and the narrowing's docstring states the ground that holds (the empty-value demonstrations are backticked prose). `spliced_copy` is the per-substitution counting splice extracted from `m061_mutant`, which now calls it; `m067_mutant` applies it to a copy of the test modules. Sixteen plants under `--self-test`: an escaped quote read one way by the repaired scan and as a manufactured `key=` by the scan with `ESCAPE` spliced out; a `.index` div ahead of two marks paired right by the repair and wrong by the ordinal pairing spliced back in; the form sentence edited alone, a row added alone, the sentence removed, a non-number word, zero; an absent page named as the page it is, a call with no `--`, one with two.

**Decisions:** none cross-cutting. Milestone-local (implementation gate): `--` as the docs-mode separator; a word table plus digits for the form count; the escaped quote read without its backslash, as pandoc 3.11 reads it.
