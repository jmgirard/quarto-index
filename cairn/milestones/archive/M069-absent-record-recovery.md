# M069: A chapter no render has written a record for reaches the book index where its terms would otherwise be lost

**Status:** done (2026-09-02, PR #69 https://github.com/jmgirard/quarto-index/pull/69)

**Goal:** In an HTML book, a chapter for which no render has written a sidecar record has its own source read by the chapter that prints an index section, so a book rendered into a tree whose records cannot be written prints an index carrying every chapter's terms rather than one short of them.

**Outcome:** `store_read` in `_extensions/index/modules/book.lua` takes a third argument, `recover_absent`, saying whether a record no listing carries may be recovered from its chapter's source in this chapter. `html_book` builds it as `#marker > 0 or ctx.position == #ctx.chapters` — a chapter carrying a placement marker of its own, and the book's last chapter, which takes on every index no marker names — settled from `resolve_markers` and the render list before the store is opened, so no two chapters of one render can disagree and the gate asks the store nothing. On that branch the record joins the existing recovery path and draws a fourth wording naming it as one no render has written; a never-written record whose source parses and reaches no mark is silent, since a chapter that marks nothing is indistinguishable from it; one whose source cannot be read draws the existing wording for that outcome. Every other chapter reads such a record as absent exactly as before. Suite: five per-chapter legs over a store-less copy of `examples/book-placement/` with the store asserted gone around every render (three recovering with href-form manifests, two silent negative controls), two AC5 legs, a whole-book control over `examples/book`, and five `--self-test` plants each shown red first — 631 checks plain, 1182 with `--self-test`; `warn-distinct.py` 81 → 82. Prose in `site/books.qmd`, `CHANGELOG.md` and DESIGN's recovery section; the books-page claim ledger 23 → 27 rows.

**Decisions:** D-045, gating recovery of an absent record on the reading chapter printing a section, superseding D-041's never-fires-on-an-absent-record clause and restating D-043's and D-044's falsifier against the gate.
