# M065: The forms a recovered chapter's marks take are fenced

**Status:** done (2026-08-31, PR #65 https://github.com/jmgirard/quarto-index/pull/65)

**Goal:** The source-recovery route M064 ships is held to the mark forms an author actually writes and to every way a record goes unusable, rather than to the one bare form and the one cause M064's fixture carries.

**Outcome:** `four.qmd` gained seven forms in eight marks — `entry="Woodwork!Joinery"`, `sort="Abacus"` on `Zephyr`, `see=`, `see-also=`, a `range=` pair on `Ingot`, `mention="principal"` on `Jetty` — each on a term no other chapter indexes, and the five term manifests carrying its share of `gamma` re-baselined from four entries to eleven. `recover_record` now carries the chapter's DECLARED sort keys in `build_record`'s declared-key-per-printed-path shape, first mark in document order winning, so a recovered `Zephyr` files under A as it does on the record route. `store_directory_unusable` tells an existing-but-unlistable store directory from an absent record — unusable when the directory cannot be listed and its own name is in a listing of its parent, probed once per rendering chapter — so a store replaced by a file recovers every chapter instead of reading as a book never rendered. The whole recovered `gamma` section is held row by row in href form by `check_index_sections`, and a new `check_locator_role` settles the role half against the record route's own principal locator as its control. Five per-field mutants under `--self-test` fence each recovered field on its own: folded levels, emptied markers, dropped sort keys, a carried-and-re-paired `range=` asserted to change nothing, and a disabled store-directory probe. `site/books.qmd` grows a fifth not-returned bullet and the store-directory case; `CHANGELOG.md` the same two facts; KI205 and KI214 narrow again, both now standing only where the store directory is absent or still lists.

**Decisions:** D-043 (an existing-but-unlistable store directory is not an absent record; extends D-041). Milestone-local: a recovered record carries the chapter's declared sort keys — a value the author typed sits inside D-041's boundary as the printed levels do, while the RESOLVED key stays outside as that chapter's own conclusion (D-009, D-021).
