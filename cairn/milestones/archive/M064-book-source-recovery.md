# M064: A chapter's terms reach the book index when its record cannot be read

**Status:** done (2026-08-30, PR #64 https://github.com/jmgirard/quarto-index/pull/64)

**Goal:** In an HTML book, a chapter whose sidecar record the building chapter opened but could not use contributes its authored terms and its placement markers to the book's index anyway, recovered by parsing that chapter's own source.

**Outcome:** `store_read`'s two unusable branches — undecodable, structurally refused, version-skewed — call `recover_record`, which reads `<root>/<chapter>` and `pandoc.read`s it inside one `pcall` and walks the parse with the extension's own mark reader. It returns the author's values alone: printed levels, surviving cross-reference targets, index name, and the chapter's top-level placement markers deduped per index. No anchor, role, pairing verdict or sort key. `drop_conditional` takes every `.content-visible`/`.content-hidden` block and span out whole before anything is read (D-042). A recovered mark is flagged `page_locator`; `chapter_href` derives its page from the chapter's own `output-file:` or its source stem, and `html.lua`'s `mark_target` emits a bare page href with no fragment. `build_entry_tree` prints one locator per destination and role. Recovered markers reach `marker_chapter`, so `first` is settled from the book's chapters rather than only from readable records (KI214). Both record reports split three ways — recovered, parsed-but-no-mark, source-unreadable; `warn-distinct.py`'s EXPECTED moves 77 to 81. An ABSENT record is untouched, so a first render is what `site/books.qmd` documents. D-041 (supersedes D-040's declined clause) and D-042 stand; KI205 and KI214 narrow to the absent record, KI216-KI220 added.

**Decisions:** D-041 and D-042 (cross-cutting, in DECISIONS.md). Milestone-local: recovery leaves a chapter's declared sort keys alone (M065 fences the richer forms); the existing unreadable-record report is extended with a clause rather than joined by a second warning; one guard around the whole read-parse-walk, proven by AC5, rather than an IP2 escalation.

T9-T12 repaired all four, with AC6 and one Scope bullet amended through the gate. Round 2 re-ran everything: all seven criteria green (562 checks plain, 1038 with `--self-test`, both exit 0), consistency gate clean, blame-history and prior-review lenses finding nothing, the diff-bug lens ten, none meeting the return floor. Two fixed at the gate: the version-skewed no-marks report wording shipped with no test (now `m064-stalenomarks`, shown red against a mutated report branch), and three over-long prose lines rewrapped. Recorded rather than fixed: KI219 (a non-markdown chapter parsed as markdown, its terms silently refiled), KI220 (the no-marks report says nothing about markers recovered), KI216 extended with an `output-file:` whose stem carries a dot, KI218 extended with a chapter declaring its own indexes. Two rejected, two to follow-up rows. No lesson added or retired.
